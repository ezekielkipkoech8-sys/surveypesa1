How payouts actually work

  • Minimum payout is KSh 500 — there is no expiry on balances below this.
  • Survey spots are limited by the research partner's quota, shown above as "spots filled."
  • Applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours; approval isn't guaranteed if your profile doesn't match the partner's screening criteria.
  • "Opening soon" surveys have no guaranteed reward amount until the partner confirms final terms.
