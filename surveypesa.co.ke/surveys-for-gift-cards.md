## The Real Problem with Gift Cards in Kenya





Let’s be honest. Gift cards create more problems than they solve for most Kenyans.





### 1. Limited Usability





Most gift cards:






- Work only in specific countries




- Require foreign accounts




- Have restrictions





For example:






- Amazon gift cards may require a US account




- Some services don’t ship to Kenya





So you’re stuck with value you can’t easily use.





### 2. Forced Spending





Gift cards don’t give you freedom.





You must:






- Spend within a specific store




- Buy things you may not need





Ever been forced to buy something just because you had a voucher? Exactly.





### 3. Hidden Loss of Value





Many people end up:






- Selling gift cards at a discount




- Trading them for cash at lower rates





So a Ksh 1000 value might become:






- Ksh 700–800 in real cash





That’s money lost.





### 4. Delays and Complications





Redeeming gift cards often involves:






- Verification steps




- Account linking




- Region restrictions





It turns a simple reward into a process.
